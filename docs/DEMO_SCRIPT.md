# TerraQuest AI — 60–90 Second Video Demo Script

> **Video Title:** TerraQuest AI: Less scrolling. More exploring. (Hacktoberfest 2026 Touch Grass Demo)  
> **Target Duration:** 75 seconds  
> **Theme:** Touch Grass &bull; Open-Weight AI &bull; Mindful Exploration

---

### [00:00 – 00:12] Introduction & The Problem
- **Visual:** Screen opens on `http://localhost:3000` with light mode; cursor smoothly scrolls past the hero headline *"Your next great adventure starts outside."*
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"We spend over 7 hours a day staring into glowing rectangles, feeling mentally drained. Most AI tools compete for even more of your screen time. TerraQuest AI is built on the opposite principle: AI creates the plan, but you experience the physical world."*

---

### [00:12 – 00:26] The Adventure Generator
- **Visual:** Click **"Find My Adventure"** to enter `/create`. User selects `15 min`, clicks `Mindful Walking`, picks `Relaxed`, enters optional setting `Riverside Trail`, and clicks **"Generate Outdoor Mission"**.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"In seconds, TerraQuest queries Google's open-weight Gemma model to craft an actionable, grounded mission tailored to your exact free time and location."*

---

### [00:26 – 00:40] Reviewing the Mission Card
- **Visual:** The generated card appears: shows steps summing to 15 minutes, preparation tips, safety notes, and the **Screen-Free Challenge** box. Cursor highlights the Gemma Open-Weight source badge.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"No endless chat threads. You get a crisp mission card, equipment essentials, sensory prompts, and a screen-free challenge. If the API is offline, our deterministic engine ensures zero disruption."*

---

### [00:40 – 00:54] Distraction-Free Mission Mode
- **Visual:** Click **"Start Distraction-Free Adventure"** to enter `/adventure/[id]`. The timer starts. Toggle **"Screen-Free Mode"** to switch into high-contrast dark forest outdoor view.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Once outside, tap into Distraction-Free Mode. Oversized text, high-contrast sunlight readability, and a clear prompt to put your phone in your pocket and tune into nature."*

---

### [00:54 – 01:05] Finishing & The Nature Journal
- **Visual:** User clicks through steps and taps **"Finish Adventure"**. Confetti bursts across the screen. User clicks **"Record Nature Reflection in Journal"** to enter `/journal`. Quickly types what they noticed and rates it 5 stars.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"After touching grass, record what you noticed in your private Nature Journal. Did your breathing slow? What bird did you hear? Everything stays 100% private in your browser."*

---

### [01:05 – 01:15] Dashboard & Outro
- **Visual:** Open **"My Adventures"** (`/my-adventures`), showcasing the verified outdoor minutes counter incrementing. Fast cut to the theme toggle switching between forest and dark mode.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Real outdoor minutes. Zero algorithms. Built with Gemma open-weight AI for Hacktoberfest 2026. Less scrolling. More exploring."*
